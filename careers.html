How we work

Our values are the job description.

We hire for three things before any skill on a résumé. You will see them in every review, incident and launch.

VAL.01

Vigilance

Always watching, never anxious.

You notice the small thing early. You would rather flag a risk on Tuesday than explain an incident on Friday, and you say it calmly.

VAL.02

Precision

Every pixel, packet and word is deliberate.

You measure before you claim, write it down before you ship, and treat accuracy as part of the craft rather than a final check.

VAL.03

Resilience

Systems that bend and recover.

You design for failure, recover fast, and run blameless reviews. You hold up in the incident room and in the boardroom.

How we hire

Four steps. No surprises.

The same process for every role, so you always know what comes next and how long it takes.

1

Intro call

30 minutes

A conversation about your work, the role and what the watch looks like day to day.

2

Work sample

About 3 hours

A realistic task from the team you'd join, done on your own time. No trick questions.

3

Team loop

Half a day

Walk us through your sample, then meet the people you'd work with every week.

4

Decision

Within a week

A clear yes or no with reasons, and an offer you can compare line by line.
